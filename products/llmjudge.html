Interaction & Chat

Manages real-time conversational sessions, from simple exchange to detailed debugging.

  • Standard Streaming Executes a continuous stream chat with the LLM.
  • Detailed Debugging --trace Captures complete logs for every call (Generator Prompt, Judge Prompt, Temperature).
  • Continued Conversation --continue Feeds the model with past conversational history.

Verification & Scoring lab verify

Evaluates pre-existing or system-generated content without requiring new generation.

  • Static Judging Evaluates a written document (.md) against defined criteria.
  • RAG Verification Simulates verification using a question and a local knowledge base (chunks.json).
