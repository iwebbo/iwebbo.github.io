How it works

Connect once, chat anywhere.

01 · CONNECT

Add providers

Hosted APIs or local runtimes. Models are discovered dynamically per provider.

02 · CHAT

Streamed, with fallback

Responses arrive over SSE with auto-reconnect, and fall back to another provider when one fails.

03 · TUNE

Templates and temperature

Per-conversation temperature, reusable prompt templates with placeholders, and a deep reasoning mode.

04 · REVIEW

History and analytics

Full-text search over stored conversations, token totals by day, provider and model, and export.

Rate limiting and multi-origin CORS are built in, so the same backend serves web, mobile and desktop clients.
